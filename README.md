## 👋 Hi there

I'm a developer based in Toulouse. I split my time between **desktop apps**, **tools for 3D artists**, **AI** and **content creation**. I like small, well-finished tools that do one thing well, and projects that teach me something new.

Right now I'm mostly working on:

- 🎓 **LessonFolk**: learning AI with an AI as your tutor
- 🧊 **Voxel Shift** and **Moodcrate**: apps for 3D artists
- 🌧️ **Ambience videos**: cabins in the rain, blizzard shelters, cabins in orbit
- 🏠 **An atlas of houses** built on French open data
